Settings

Footage never leaves your device. It is stored on this phone only.

To install: on iPhone tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android open the browser menu, then Install app.

Back camera

Plays where the live view would be when the camera opens.

Front camera

Plays after you tap the flip button. Leave it empty to keep showing the back camera footage.
